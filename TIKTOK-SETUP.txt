BORDER BEATS → TIKTOK: ONE-TIME SETUP
======================================

What you get: log in with TikTok once, and every video you render (Export MP4 or Render all) can
go straight to your TikTok account with its caption. TikTok requires a short confirmation screen
for each post (you pick who can see it and press Post) - that screen opens by itself when the
videos finish.

TikTok only lets registered apps post, so you register your own (free) app first. Takes ~20 min.


1. DEVELOPER ACCOUNT
   - Go to https://developers.tiktok.com and log in with your TikTok account.
   - Accept the developer terms.

2. CREATE THE APP  (Manage apps -> Connect an app)
   - App name: Border Beats   (the name must not contain "TikTok")
   - Category: Entertainment
   - Description: "Renders physics/music videos on my PC and posts them to my TikTok account."
   - App icon: border-beats-app-icon.png (in this folder, 1024 x 1024)
   - Website, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy URLs (GitHub Pages, from the docs folder):
       https://kkinnit-o8.github.io/BoarderBeats/
       https://kkinnit-o8.github.io/BoarderBeats/terms.html
       https://kkinnit-o8.github.io/BoarderBeats/privacy.html
     (Replace [your email] in docs/privacy.html with a contact address first.)
   - Platform: Desktop.

3. ADD THE PRODUCTS  (in the app: Add products)
   - Login Kit  -> platform Desktop -> Redirect URI:   http://127.0.0.1:8743/callback/
     (exactly like that, with the slash at the end)
   - Content Posting API -> switch ON "Direct Post".
   - Scopes: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload

4. SANDBOX  (lets you use the app without TikTok's review)
   - Switch the app to Sandbox -> Create Sandbox -> Apply changes.
   - Sandbox settings -> Target users -> add your own TikTok account, and accept when TikTok asks.

5. YOUR KEYS
   - Double-click "Start Border Beats.bat" once. It creates tiktok-keys.json in this folder.
   - Open tiktok-keys.json in Notepad and paste the SANDBOX "Client key" and "Client secret"
     from the developer portal between the quotes. Save.
   - Keep this file private. Never send it to anyone (including in chats).

6. LOG IN
   - Start Border Beats with "Start Border Beats.bat" (keep its black window open). It opens
     http://127.0.0.1:8743/ - always use Border Beats from that address when you want to post.
   - Open "Post to TikTok" on the start screen -> Log in with TikTok -> allow.

7. POSTING
   - Render videos as usual. When they finish, the posting screen opens: check the caption,
     choose who can see each video, press Post.
   - "How videos go to TikTok" (in the Post to TikTok section):
       * Posted to your profile: the caption goes with it.
       * Sent to your TikTok inbox as drafts: you finish and post each one in the TikTok app
         (the caption is copied for you to paste). Useful while your app isn't approved (below).


IMPORTANT LIMITS (TikTok's rules, not this app's)
- Until TikTok approves your app ("Direct Post audit"), posts can only be "Only me", and your
  TikTok account must be set to Private while posting (TikTok app -> Settings and privacy ->
  Privacy -> Private account). Afterwards set the account public again and change each video
  to "Everyone". The inbox/drafts option avoids this: you post publicly from the app yourself
  (if TikTok allows drafts for sandbox apps - try it).
- Approval: in the developer portal you can submit the app for review and the Direct Post
  audit. TikTok states that apps for private or personal use aren't approved, so expect a no.
- About 15 posts per day per account; at most 5 drafts waiting in your inbox; videos must fit
  your account's length limit (usually up to 10 minutes).
- The login renews itself for a year; after that, log in again.

If something goes wrong, the message on the posting screen says what TikTok answered.
